Important

737 FMC Trainer is for training and familiarisation only. It is not for real-world navigation, flight planning or the operation of any aircraft. Performance figures are approximations, not manufacturer data; the approved documents of your aircraft and operator always take precedence.

The app is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The Boeing Company or any manufacturer of flight management systems. Boeing and 737 are trademarks of their owner and are used only to describe the airplane the app is about.

Subscription

  • 737 FMC Trainer on the App Store is used with an auto-renewable monthly subscription. A new subscriber may be offered a free trial; when it ends, the subscription starts automatically unless cancelled before.
  • Payment is charged to the Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • Subscriptions can be managed and cancelled in the Apple Account settings on the device, or from Settings → Subscription in the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes what the app does with data. It is short because the app does very little with it.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app holds is stored on the device itself, inside the app’s own storage area: the settings, the panel layout, the flight in progress and its timeline, lesson progress, and the flights and scenarios the user saves. It is removed when the app is deleted, and is included in the device’s own backups if the user has those switched on.

What is transmitted

  • iCloud. Saved flights and scenarios are kept in the user’s own iCloud Drive through Apple’s iCloud service, so that the user’s devices signed in to the same Apple Account share them. The developer has no access to this data. iCloud can be switched off for the app in the device settings.
  • Linked devices. When the user links devices to fly one flight on several screens, the app sends the flight state directly between those devices over the local network or a peer-to-peer connection, encrypted, after the user confirms a pairing code. Nothing passes through a server.
  • Maps. The map for choosing a parking stand or a runway shows Apple Maps imagery, which the device loads from Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies to it.
  • Purchases. The subscription is bought, renewed and managed by Apple through the App Store. The app only learns whether a subscription is active; it never sees payment details.

The app does not use location services, the camera, the microphone or contacts.

Children

The app is a training tool for pilots and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something else with data, this page will say so before that version is released.
